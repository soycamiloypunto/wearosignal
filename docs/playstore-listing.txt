[APP TITLE] (max 30 chars — 28 used)
WearOSignal — Wear OS Client

[SHORT DESCRIPTION] (max 80 chars — 73 used)
Unofficial Signal client for Wear OS: chats, voice notes, emojis & media.

[FULL DESCRIPTION] (max 4000 chars)
WearOSignal is an independent, open-source Signal client built specifically for Wear OS smartwatches (compatible with Samsung Galaxy Watch 4 and newer, Pixel Watch, and Wear OS 3+ devices).

Link your watch as a secondary device via QR code (just like Signal Desktop) to read and reply to your conversations directly from your wrist — even when your phone stays behind.

KEY FEATURES
• Clean chat list & pinned chats: browse 1:1 and group conversations with decrypted contact/group photos, message previews, rotary bezel/crown scrolling, and pin up to 3 favorite chats to the top.
• 3-button composer bar:
  - Voice notes: record AAC/M4A voice messages with a live microphone waveform and play received/sent audio right on your watch.
  - 9 customizable favorite emojis: send with one tap and customize all 9 slots anytime.
  - Text & voice dictation (Aa): reply using Wear OS keyboard or speech-to-text.
• 5 customizable quick replies: scroll below the composer bar to send your favorite replies in one tap.
• Full media viewing: view photos, WebP stickers, animated GIFs, MP4 videos, and play voice notes natively on your watch.
• Disappearing messages & synced deletions: automatically purges expired messages matching your Signal chat timer and syncs "Delete for me", "Delete for all", and chat deletions with your phone.
• Smart contacts picker: clearly separates watch contacts into "On Signal" and "Not on Signal".
• Modern Wear OS Settings: interactive checkboxes for notifications (1/5/15 min), read receipts, private lock screen, clear local chats, and one-tap unlink.
• Tile & Complication: check recent chats and unread count right from your watch face.
• 5 languages supported automatically: English, Spanish, French, Portuguese, and Italian.

PRIVACY & SECURITY
• Direct end-to-end encrypted connection exclusively to official Signal servers (*.signal.org).
• Zero intermediary servers, zero ads, zero analytics or trackers.
• Local account credentials and identity keys encrypted at rest with Android Keystore (AES-256-GCM).
• Open-source under GNU AGPLv3: https://github.com/soycamiloypunto/wearosignal

DISCLAIMER
WearOSignal is an independent, unofficial client for Signal and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Signal Messenger, LLC. An active Signal account on your phone is required to link your watch. Choose "Don't transfer messages" if prompted during linking.
